# Step 07: Documentation

## Goal
Three documents so the demo can be reproduced, delivered, and defended.

## Plan
- `REPRODUCE.md`: from clone to table, every command, expected runtime per
  step on M2 Max and on Colab L4.
- `DEMO.md`: 30-minute run of show, what to type when, what to say, backup
  clips, the table.
- `EXPLAINER.md`: how a decision model differs from a generator; the pointer
  head; LoRA; what fine-tuning changed; calibration and Brier; why one forward
  pass is fast; what the balloon test measures; limits. Written at the level
  of "I can defend this in front of ML engineers".
- `FAQ.md`: hard questions the audience will ask, with short answers.
- `slides/outline.md`: slide-by-slide outline with speaker notes.

## Acceptance
- Review by a second reader for technical accuracy against the Kev source
  and for claims not backed by our own measurements.
